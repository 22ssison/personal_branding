01 / Profile

Who is Samantha?

Kia ora, I'm Samantha, also known as 'Sammy'. I am a Year 13 student at Wakatipu High School with a passion for technology, finance, leadership, and creating meaningful impact within my community.

Born in the Philippines and raised in New Zealand, I have always been curious about how systems work—whether that's financial markets, emerging technologies, organisations, or communities.

I actively seek opportunities that challenge me to grow. From leading students as Deputy Head Girl, speaking alongside CEOs at the Queenstown Women in Business Conference, developing software projects, and contributing youth perspectives to regional initiatives, I believe growth happens when you step forward before you feel completely ready.
